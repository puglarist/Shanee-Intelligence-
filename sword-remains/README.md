## PRIORITY SPECIES (Phase 3 Deep Dive)

### 🌿 Ginseng (*Panax quinquefolius*)
**The sacred herb. Deep roots. Sovereignty symbol.**

- Root depth: 12-18 inches (deep taproot systems)
- Growth timeline: 4-7 years to harvest
- Appalachian significance: Trade commodity, medicinal foundation
- Restoration potential: Cultivated propagation restores what was wildcrafted

### 🌿 Yellow Root / Goldenseal (*Hydrastis canadensis*)
**The golden restoration. Ecosystem indicator.**

- Root depth: 6-8 inches (rhizome system)
- Growth timeline: 5-6 years to sustainable harvest
- Medicinal uses: Immune support, traditional remedy
- Restoration framework: Forest understory cultivation, agriforestry integration

### 🌿 Virginia Snakeroot (*Aristolochia serpentaria*)
**The winding knowledge. Historical Appalachian remedy.**

- Root depth: 8-12 inches
- Growth timeline: 3-4 years
- Historical use: Snakebite treatment, traditional medicine
- Research opportunity: Redocumentation of propagation methods

### 🌿 Complementary Species
Bloodroot, trillium, ramps, native mints, and other Appalachian botanicals forming integrated cultivation systems.

## HOW TO CONTRIBUTE

We operate on **full community contribution**. Anyone can submit research.

### Contributing Research
1. Visit `/research.html`
2. Click "Submit Research" 
3. Choose category: species, cultivation, soil science, agriforestry, traditional wisdom
4. Fill form with:
   - Title & description
   - Scientific/common names
   - Your experience level & location
   - Sources or citations
   - Growing conditions, results, lessons learned
5. Submit for community review

### Contributing Code/Design
1. Fork the repository
2. Create feature branch: `feature/your-research-area`
3. Add or improve content in `/pages`, `/data`, `/scripts`
4. Submit pull request with clear description
5. Community reviews and votes on quality

### Becoming a Curator
Long-term contributors may become research curators, helping validate and organize community submissions. Governance is transparent and community-driven.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Is this about commercial farming?**
A: No. This is about sovereignty, food security, medicinal access, and restoration. Commercial is one expression, but community-centered growing is the priority.

**Q: Can I submit incomplete research?**
A: Yes. In-progress research, questions, and observations are valuable. The community refines together.

**Q: What about traditional/Indigenous knowledge?**
A: Deeply honored and centered. If you're sharing ancestral knowledge, please note that. We credit and respect origins.

**Q: How do I know if research is reliable?**
A: Community voting and curator review. Contributors build reputation over time. Sources are cited. Conflicting info sparks discussion, not suppression.

**Q: Can I grow these plants commercially?**
A: That's between you and your ethics. This platform supports all expressions—subsistence, small farm, commercial. We just document what works.
